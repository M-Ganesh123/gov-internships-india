# Government Internship Opportunities ✨

Explore prestigious internship programs offered by various Government of India ministries and bodies. These opportunities provide valuable exposure, stipends, and professional growth across diverse sectors.

| S.No. | Internship Name & Organising Body | Stipend | Duration / Notes | Application Link |
|-------|-----------------------------------|---------|------------------|------------------|
| 1 | Prime Minister Internship Scheme (via Ministry of Corporate Affairs) | ₹5,000/month + one-time grant ₹6,000 *(ClearTax)* | 12-month internship in companies across sectors *(ClearTax)* | [Apply here](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmis) |
| 2 | DPIIT Internship Scheme (via Department for Promotion of Industry & Internal Trade) | ₹10,000/month *(myscheme.gov.in)* | Duration: 1–3 months (typically) | [Apply here](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/dpiit-is) |
| 3 | MEA Internship Programme (via Ministry of External Affairs) | ₹10,000/month + airfare reimbursement *(internship.mea.gov.in)* | Duration: 1–3 months; posted at Delhi HQ | [Apply here](https://internship.mea.gov.in/internship) |
| 4 | Digital India Internship Scheme (via Ministry of Electronics & IT) | ~₹10,000/month *(unstop.com)* | Duration: minimum 2 months; up to ~3 months | [Apply here](https://intern.meity.gov.in/) |
| 5 | National Renewable Energy Internship (via Ministry of New & Renewable Energy) | ₹15,000/month (physical internships) | Duration: preferably 2–6 months | [Apply here](https://mnre.gov.in/en/national-renewable-energy-internship-programme-2/) |
| 6 | MWCD Internship (via Ministry of Women and Child Development) | Lump-sum ₹20,000/month *(WCD NIC)* | Batch-based; hostel support in Delhi | [Apply here](https://wcd.intern.nic.in/) |
| 7 | SEBI ITD Internship (via Securities & Exchange Board of India) | ~₹15,000/month (tentative) | Duration: 2–6 months (IT/tech streams) | [Apply here](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/about/AboutAction.do?doInternships=yes) |
| 8 | National Centre for Good Governance Internship | ~₹10,000/month (honorarium) | Duration depends on project; not always fixed | [Apply here](https://ncgg.org.in/training/) |
| 9 | Forest & Climate Change Internship (via MoEFCC) | ₹10,000/month *(Govt schemes)* | Duration: up to 3 months | [Apply here](https://moef.gov.in/en/about-the-ministry/internship/) |
| 10 | NITI Aayog Internship Scheme | Unpaid *(Govt Internship)* | Duration: 6 weeks to 6 months; apply 1–10 every month | [Apply here](https://www.niti.gov.in/internship) |

> **Note:** Stipends, durations, and eligibility criteria are subject to change. Always verify details on the official application portal before applying.
